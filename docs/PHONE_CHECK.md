# CapyTube phone check

This is a test site. The coins are play coins, not money. Nothing is charged in money.
Your display name and chat messages are public on the test site.
After the test the team deletes your test accounts and everything they wrote.

Do these steps on a real iPhone and a real Android phone. Use a new account on each.
Open https://dev.capytube.xyz. If a step fails, record it and continue where possible.

1. **Sign up.** Tap **Sign in** at the top. On the sign-in page, choose the option to create an account.
   Use your own email address. Set a password of at least 12 characters when asked.
   Enter the verification code sent by email. Finish the prompts; sign in if asked.
   You should return to the site signed in.

2. **Coins and name.** Check that the top coin balance is **50**.
   In **Pick a display name**, enter 2–32 characters in **Name**. Tap **Save**.
   Expect **Display name saved.** and the prompt to close.
   Tap **Me** to open **Profile**. Check your name under **Display name**.

3. **Vote.** Tap **Play**. Under **Choose your capybara**, tap **Magnus**.
   Select **Carrots**, or another listed snack. Leave **Votes** at **1**. Read **Cost**; the starting price is 1 play coin per vote.
   Tap **Vote**, check the cost in **Check and confirm**, then tap **Confirm**.
   Expect **Thank you for your vote!** and the balance to fall by that cost.

4. **Bid.** On **Play**, tap **Elon** and find **Name the next climbing hold**.
   Leave **Your bid** at **Minimum next bid**, 1 above the current top bid. The page fills it in.
   Tap **Bid**, check the cost, then **Confirm**. Expect **Thank you for your bid!** and the new top bid.
   The bid leaves your balance, and comes back if someone outbids you (your second phone will).
   If the minimum bid is more than your balance, write down both numbers and go on to step 5.

5. **Free camera.** Tap **Watch**. Open a capybara card and select **Main cam** or **Food cam**.
   If neither appears, go back and choose another card. Tap the video's play control.
   Expect moving video marked **Recorded**. In **Chat**, tap **Love** once; its count should rise.
   Type one short line in **Message** and tap **Send**. Expect your line and display name. Both actions are free.

6. **Paid camera.** Return to **Watch**. Open **Elon**'s card. Select **Climbing wall cam**.
   Read the price: initially **6 play coins a minute**. Tap **Watch**, then **Start watching**. Tap video play if needed.
   Keep the page visible for one minute. Expect moving video and a lower balance.
   The next minute is bought about 40 seconds after starting, so this check normally costs 12 play coins.
   Tap **Stop watching**, not video pause. Expect **Stopped. Nothing more will be charged.** and the video to close.
   Note the balance. Wait another minute, then reload. It must not fall further from camera charges.

7. **Coin history.** Tap **Me**. On **Profile**, scroll to **Ledger**; tap **Load more** if shown.
   Find **Welcome grant**, **Snack vote**, **Bid** and **Paid camera minute**, with their amounts and dates.
   On the first phone, **Bid returned (outbid)** appears only after the second phone bids in step 4:
   if you test the phones one after the other, check for it on the first phone at the end.
   Expect two camera entries if the early renewal happened. Check the amounts against your spending.

8. **Sign out.** Tap **Sign out** at the top. Expect the home page and **Sign in** back at the top.
   The coin balance should disappear. Tap **Me**; **Profile** should say **Signed out** and hide your ledger.

9. **Rotate.** Tap **Watch** and reopen a free camera as in step 5. Start the video.
   Turn off the phone's rotation lock. Rotate from portrait to landscape and back.
   Check that the layout adapts, video keeps playing, and no video, text, or controls are cut off.

10. **Offline reload.** Turn on airplane mode and turn off Wi-Fi. Reload the page.
    Note exactly what appears; a browser connection error is possible. There is no promised offline page.
    Turn airplane mode off, restore your connection, and reload. The site should return and the free video should play when tapped.

## What to send back

For each phone, send:
- Phone model, OS version, browser, and browser version.
- Pass or fail for each numbered step. For every failure, add one line explaining what happened.
- A screenshot of every failure.
- The test date and time, including your time zone.
