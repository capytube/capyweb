Brand story

One bird. One ticket. A calm cup.

Capycoffee is an online roast bay, not a café with a chalkboard of twelve blends. You fill a form the way you’d slide a ticket under the glass at Ichiran. We roast that exact condition, then pack whole beans.

Meet Freebird

Freebird is the only SKU on the bar. Right now it is Typica & Catimor from Chiang Mai, Thailand, washed at 1,200–1,500m. Packed as whole beans. When the lot is gone, a new origin lands under the same name. You always order Freebird. The passport changes.

Why a form?

Capybaras do not rush. Neither do we. The order form is the whole conversation: roast profile, roast level, bag, optional tasting cup. Whole beans only — no grind menu. A native app can reuse the same ticket later — same fields, same three named profiles, same Freebird from Chiang Mai.
